Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 3–5 business days. You will get tracking details by email.

What is your return policy?

You can return any product within 30 days in its original condition for a full refund.

How do I claim a warranty repair?

Contact us with your order number and a short description of the issue. We will arrange a repair or replacement.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, within 2 hours of placing it. After that, contact support and we will do our best to help.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards, bank transfer and mobile payments.
